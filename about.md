My name is Evgeny Manzhosov. I work at Amazon, where I develop methodologies to
screen for silent data corruption and assess test coverage in Trainium
hardware. More broadly, I am interested in system security and reliability,
hardware support for security, privacy, and compilers.

My interest in reliability grew out of my Ph.D. research at Columbia
University, where I worked with Professor
[Simha Sethumadhavan](http://www.cs.columbia.edu/~simha/) and earned my degree
in May 2025. At the beginning of my Ph.D., I worked on memory safety and
pointer integrity, gradually drifting toward the intersection of system
security and reliability. My research explored these questions in both
general-purpose and specialized computing, including machine learning and ML
with homomorphic encryption. Alongside these topics, I am interested in
program analysis and hardware/software co-design, particularly where they
connect with questions of security and reliability.

My time at Columbia began before my Ph.D., with a master’s in Electrical
Engineering. During that period, I worked on several research projects in the
Lightwave Research Laboratory under the supervision of Professor Keren
Bergman. I completed my M.Sc. with an Award of Excellence before joining
Professor Sethumadhavan’s lab for my doctoral studies.

Before Columbia, I studied at the Technion – Israel Institute of Technology
from fall 2008 to spring 2014, earning a B.Sc. in Physics and a B.Sc. in
Electrical Engineering. Like many students at the Technion, I combined my
studies with work in industry. I started working part-time at Intel in 2011,
then moved to Apple in 2013. In fact, I was the first student employee at Apple
Israel. Later that same year, I transitioned to a full-time role while
finishing my undergraduate studies. I stayed at Apple until 2016, when I
joined Cisco, and a year later returned to university to begin my studies at
Columbia.

The Technion is also where I picked up an interest that has stayed with me
outside of work: analog photography. I started shooting film during my
undergraduate studies and have been doing it ever since.
